Frequently Asked Questions

Get clear answers to the most common questions about our non-invasive slab leak detection service in McKinney, TX.

We use a combination of specialized acoustic sensors, infrared thermal imaging, and pressure diagnostics to “listen” and “see” what’s happening under your slab from the surface. Acoustic sensors pick up the distinct sound pattern of pressurized water escaping from a pipe. Thermal cameras detect subtle temperature changes where hot or cold water is migrating through concrete. By overlaying these readings with the layout of your plumbing, we can pinpoint likely leak locations—typically within a 2–3 foot radius—without making a single hole in your floors.
For typical residential slab leaks in McKinney, we routinely achieve a localization window of about ±3 feet, which is precise enough for plumbers to limit cutting to a small, targeted area instead of opening multiple sections of slab. After every visit, we provide a written report that includes our readings, suspected leak location, and recommended access point. Most plumbers welcome this information because it reduces their exploratory time and gives them a clear starting plan. If your plumber has questions, we’re happy to speak with them directly about our findings.
Common warning signs include: unusually high water bills, warm or cold spots on tile or hardwood floors, the sound of running water when all fixtures are off, hairline cracks in walls or floors that appear or grow suddenly, damp carpet along interior walls, or a constantly running water meter. If you notice one or more of these symptoms—especially in an older McKinney home with copper lines—it’s smart to schedule a non-invasive scan. Catching a slab leak early typically means a smaller repair and far less risk to your foundation.
We focus exclusively on detection and diagnostics—we do not perform plumbing repairs or foundation work. This separation is intentional and part of our commitment to unbiased recommendations. After we identify the leak location and provide a detailed report, you are free to work with any licensed plumber or foundation company you choose. Many clients share our findings with multiple contractors to compare repair strategies and quotes with full confidence in the underlying data.
Coverage for slab leaks varies widely by policy. While our detection fee is typically paid out-of-pocket, the documentation we provide can be very helpful if you open a claim. Our reports clearly outline the symptoms observed, test results, suspected leak location, and recommended access point—along with photos and thermal images when applicable. Many clients submit this documentation to their adjuster to support coverage for access, repair, or water damage related to the leak. We always recommend speaking with your insurance provider about what is and isn’t covered in your specific policy.
